# How have Mental Health Scores for Youth in Canada changed over Time? 

How have Mental Health scores for Youth in Canada Changed over time? A Validated Trend Analysis of Three National Mental Health Surveys, 2002 to 2022. 


### Primary Question: 
How has self-rated mental health among Canadian youth changed from 2002 to 2022 compared with older adults, and how is it associated with protective factors such as community belonging, physical activity and spiritual beliefs?  

### Secondary Question: 
Does the data obtained through StatCan Public Use Microdata Files (PUMF) match the full survey files, accessed through StatCan Real Time Remote Access (RTRA), closely enough to support those findings? 



#### Data
The three datasets that will be used in this analysis are the following:
1. Canadian Community Health Survey, 2002: Cycle 1.2, Mental Health and Well-being
2. Canadian Community Health Survey, 2012: Mental Health
3. Mental Health and Access to Care Survey, 2022 

These three datasets will be sourced in two distinct ways.
1. Statistics Canada Public Use Microdata Files (PUMF)
  These can be accessed through the official Statcan RDS explorer: https://rich-data-services.statcan.gc.ca/rds/en/explorer
  The PUMF datasets contain microdata and scores for individuals, however they have been anonymized, and modified from their source values. 

2. Statistics Canada Real Time Remote Access (RTRA)
  RTRA data pulls from official unmodified datasets, and to access them you must submit an application as well as SAS scripts.
  A limitation of RTRA is that it is not possible to obtain microdata, or any meaningful correlations between values.
